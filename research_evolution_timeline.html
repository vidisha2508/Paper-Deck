Research Evolution Timeline

Key Turning Points
ARCHITECTURE 2017

Transformer Architecture & Self-Attention

Replaced recurrent neural networks with multi-head self-attention mechanisms, establishing the foundational paradigm for modern LLMs.

★ 120k+ Citations Vaswani et al.
BENCHMARK 2020

Scaling Laws & In-Context Learning

Demonstrated that scaling autoregressive models to 175B parameters enables zero and few-shot task execution via prompt context alone.

🔥 45k+ Citations Brown et al. (GPT-3)
METHODOLOGY 2022

RLHF & Alignment Tuning

Pioneered Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (InstructGPT) to align raw language model outputs with human intent and safety guidelines.

✓ ChatGPT Core Ouyang et al.
THEORY 2024

Direct Preference Optimization

Derived a closed-form policy mapping from reward functions directly to policy loss, bypassing complex actor-critic RL training loops.

⚡ 3.5x Faster Alignment Rafailov et al. (Stanford)
BENCHMARK 2023

Dense Retrieval & Dynamic RAG

Combined neural parametric memory with non-parametric vector databases to eliminate hallucinations and enable real-time factual grounding.

⚡ 94% Groundedness Lewis et al.
ARCHITECTURE 2025

Test-Time Compute & Chain-of-Thought

Shifted compute scaling to inference time using tree search and step-by-step verification to solve complex mathematical and logical reasoning tasks.

★ Reasoning +42% OpenAI Reasoning Lab
